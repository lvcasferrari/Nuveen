How it works

Register an NFC tag in Nuveen, put it somewhere you have to get up to reach, and set an alarm. When it rings, open Nuveen (or tap "Scan NFC" on the alarm) and hold your iPhone near the tag.

The alarm did not ring

  • Nuveen uses Apple's alarm system on iOS 26 and later. Check Settings > Nuveen and make sure Alarms and Notifications are allowed.
  • On iOS versions before 26, Nuveen falls back to notifications, which follow Silent mode and Focus.

The alarm is too loud or too quiet

Edit the alarm and change its volume, then tap Preview: it rings a short real alarm at exactly that level. The overall level also follows your iPhone's alert volume in Settings > Sounds & Haptics.

My tag is not recognised

Scan the same tag you registered, holding it near the top back of the iPhone. You can register a new tag in Settings inside the app.

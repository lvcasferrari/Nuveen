Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Nuveen does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your alarms (time, name, repeat days, volume) and settings.
  • The identifier of the NFC tags you register, used only to check that you scanned your own tag.
  • Any custom alarm sound you choose, copied into the app's private storage.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): to ring on the lock screen, in Silent mode and in Focus.
  • Notifications: local reminders and a fallback alarm. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • NFC: to read your tag when you stop an alarm.

Children

Nuveen does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
